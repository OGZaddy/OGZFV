If you want to relay the bricks for your planter boxes and make sure they’re secure and aligned, here are a few options for what to put **between the bricks** to help lock them together and prevent shifting:

---

### 1. **Polymeric Sand** (Best for dry-stacked bricks)
- **What it is**: A special sand with polymers that hardens when watered.
- **Use it if**: You’re dry-stacking the bricks (no mortar) and want a clean, semi-permanent lock between them.
- **How to use**: Brush it into the gaps between bricks, then mist it with water to activate. It sets up solid.

---

### 2. **Mortar** (Best for permanent, solid bond)
- **What it is**: A mix of cement, sand, and water that hardens and bonds bricks permanently.
- **Use it if**: You want a very solid, long-lasting structure and don’t plan to adjust them again later.
- **How to use**: Spread a layer between each brick with a trowel.

---

### 3. **Landscape Block Adhesive** (Quick and strong)
- **What it is**: A heavy-duty construction adhesive made for outdoor landscaping.
- **Use it if**: You want a strong bond without mixing

Got it — you're relaying bricks around your planter boxes, digging them up to level the ground, and you want to make sure they don’t shift or lean again. If you're not mortaring them but still want them to stay tightly together, here are your best options:

---

### **1. Landscape Block Adhesive** *(Highly Recommended)*
- **What it does**: Glues bricks together with a super-strong outdoor adhesive.
- **Why it's good**: Fast, easy, durable, and flexible enough for minor shifts in temperature or settling.
- **How to use**: 
  - Clean the brick surfaces.
  - Apply a thick bead of adhesive (like Liquid Nails Landscape Block or Loctite PL 500) between each brick as you stack.
  - Press firmly and let cure 24–48 hours.
- **Pro tip**: Works great even if you’re stacking just 1–2 rows and not mortaring.

---

### **2. Polymeric Sand** *(For gaps, not bonding edges)*
- **What it does**: Fills joints and locks bricks in place when it hardens.
- **Why it's good**: Keeps weeds and ants out, helps reduce shifting.
- **How to use**: Sweep it into cracks, then mist with water so it sets.
- **Note**: Doesn’t glue bricks together side-to-side, but helps with surface stability.

---

### **3. Mortar Mix** *(For permanent solutions)*
- **What it does**: Cements bricks together like a traditional wall.
- **Why it's good**: Super solid — won't shift at all.
- **Downside**: Takes more time, harder to redo later.

---

### **Also important:**
- **Base layer**: Make sure your foundation is **well-compacted** and level. Use crushed gravel or paver base topped with sand to prevent future slanting.
- **Level each brick**: Use a rubber mallet and level as you go — the adhesive works best if they’re flush.

---
