This plant is called **Kalanchoe daigremontiana**, commonly known as **Mother of Thousands**, **Alligator Plant**, or **Mexican Hat Plant**.

### Key Features:
- The **tiny plantlets** growing along the edges of the leaves are a giveaway — they fall off easily and root wherever they land.
- It has thick, succulent leaves, often V-shaped and toothed.
- Very hardy and spreads rapidly.

### Care Tips:
- **Light:** Prefers bright, indirect sunlight (can tolerate direct sun).
- **Water:** Let the soil dry between waterings — it’s a succulent, so avoid overwatering.
- **Soil:** Well-draining soil is essential.
- **Propagation:** Those little plantlets do all the work for you!
