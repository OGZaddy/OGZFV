### **Tactical Team Movements for Armed Defense**
**Source: *Responsibly Armed Americans, February/March 2025***

### **The Power of Two**
The power of two or more armed individuals working together is remarkable. While many readers of this magazine are solo protectors of themselves and their families, an increasing number of couples now carry concealed weapons. By learning to operate as a team, a pair of armed citizens can significantly enhance their ability to survive a lethal encounter, whether at home or in today’s increasingly dangerous streets.

### **Two Basic Movements**
You should learn and regularly practice two basic movements:  
1. **Movement to Contact**  
2. **Bounding Overwatch**

#### **Movement to Contact**  
- This involves a pair of armed individuals moving steadily toward a specific location at a controlled pace.
- One member acts as the point, scanning ahead for threats.
- The other acts as the rearguard, ensuring 360-degree situational awareness.
- Clear communication is critical to stay together and prepared for sudden attacks.

#### **Bounding Overwatch**  
- This technique divides your team into specific tasks.
- One member provides overwatch, covering the area ahead.
- The other moves forward, leapfrogging to a new position before switching roles.
- This method ensures continuous protection while advancing through potentially dangerous environments.

---

### **Inside Your Home**
- Tactical maneuvers can be effective in a home invasion scenario.
- Movement-to-contact is useful when responding to sounds of a breach, such as a kicked-in door or broken window.
- Bounding overwatch can be used in retreat if an armed intruder is present.
- Family members should be trained to take cover in bullet-resistant positions whenever possible.

---

### **While in Public**
- In a larger, more open environment such as a mall, movement-to-contact is useful for covering ground quickly while maintaining awareness.
- If an attacker opens fire, the priority should be escaping to safety while guiding unarmed civilians.
- Commands such as “follow us” can help lead others to cover while armed movement provides security.
- If an active shooter is approaching, movement-to-contact may be necessary, but only if confident in marksmanship and the ability to maintain self-control under extreme stress.
