To build a robust raised garden bed, the ideal dimensions and materials will depend on the space you have available and your gardening needs. Here’s a standard design that works well for most vegetable and flower gardens.

### **Standard Raised Bed Dimensions:**
- **Length**: 4 feet (120 cm) – this is a manageable width for reaching into the bed from either side without stepping on the soil.
- **Width**: 8 feet (240 cm) – this allows for a good amount of space for plants while being accessible.
- **Height**: 12 to 18 inches (30 to 45 cm) – tall enough to promote healthy root growth while being easy to work in without bending over too much.

### **Materials List**:
1. **Wood**:
   - **Cedar or Redwood**: Ideal for the frame because these woods are naturally resistant to rot and pests.
   - **Pressure-treated lumber**: A more affordable option, but be mindful of using non-toxic treatments if growing edible plants.

2. **Screws** or **Nails** (for assembly) – Galvanized or stainless steel screws/nails to avoid rusting.

3. **Landscape fabric** – To line the bottom of the bed (optional, but useful for preventing weeds).

4. **Soil Mix**:
   - **Topsoil**: 50% of the bed's fill.
   - **Compost**: 30% for added nutrients.
   - **Coco Coir or Peat Moss**: 20% to help with water retention.

5. **Wood Sealant or Wood Stain** (optional) – To protect the wood and prolong its lifespan.

6. **Hardware cloth** (optional) – To line the bottom if you're concerned about burrowing pests.

7. **Corner Braces** (optional) – For extra durability and sturdiness.

### **Tools You’ll Need**:
- Saw (if you need to cut the lumber to size)
- Drill and drill bits (for making holes for screws)
- Measuring tape and level
- Hammer or screwdriver
