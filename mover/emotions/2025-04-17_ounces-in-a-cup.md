Yes, **clarified butter** and **rendered butter** are essentially the same product, though the terms are used differently depending on context.

### Clarified Butter
Clarified butter is made by gently melting butter to separate and remove the milk solids and water, leaving behind pure butterfat. This process results in a golden, clear fat with a higher smoke point, making it ideal for high-heat cooking like sautéing and frying. It's also lactose-free and has a longer shelf life than regular butter. citeturn0search11

### Rendered Butter
The term "rendered butter" is less commonly used but refers to the same process as clarifying butter. Rendering typically means melting animal fat to separate impurities, and when applied to butter, it involves removing milk solids and water to obtain pure butterfat. citeturn0search9

### Conclusion
While "clarified butter" is the more commonly used term, especially in culinary contexts, "rendered butter" refers to the same product. Both involve melting butter to remove milk solids and water, resulting in pure butterfat suitable for high-heat cooking and with a longer shelf life.

### **Mamool (4 Dozen Batch – Family Easter Edition)**  
**Adapted from Grandma’s Original**

#### **Dough:**
- 3½ cups all-purpose flour  
- 1½ cups rendered butter (**ghee**, ~11–13 oz)  
- 2 eggs  
- 6 tablespoons sugar (or ⅜ cup)

#### **Filling:**
- 1 to 1½ cups finely chopped pecans (7.5 oz bag works great)  
- ¼ to ⅜ cup sugar  
- 1½ teaspoons rosewater

---

### **Instructions:**

1. **Prepare Dough:**  
   In a mixing bowl, whip ghee until light & fluffy. Add eggs and sugar, mixing well. Slowly add flour a little at a time until fully incorporated. Set aside.

2. **Prepare Filling:**  
   In a separate bowl, mix chopped pecans, sugar, and rosewater. Stir until evenly coated and aromatic.

3. **Shape Cookies:**  
   Roll dough into small balls (about walnut size). Flatten slightly, fill with pecan mixture, then pinch closed and roll into a smooth ball.  
   Press into a mamool mold or decorate by hand. Gently tap to release.

4. **Bake:**  
   Place 4 dozen cookies on baking sheets.  
   Bake at **350°F for 25–30 minutes**, until bottoms are lightly browned.  
   Do **not** overbake — they will harden as they cool.

5. **Finish:**  
   Let sit for 5 minutes after baking. Then dust generously with powdered sugar or dunk each cookie in a bowl of it for full coverage.

---

**Store:** In an airtight container, they’ll last up to 2 weeks. But they won’t last that long.
