### 🧪 Use Acetone When:
- You just drilled or cut **bare metal**
- You want to remove **oils, grease, metal dust, shavings, or coolant**
- You're about to:
  - Paint it
  - Prime it
  - Weld near it (especially TIG)
  - Epoxy or JB Weld it

---

### ⚠️ Acetone Best Practice:
- Use a **clean lint-free rag** (paper towel can leave fibers if too cheap)
- Wipe **once or twice max per rag side**, then flip to a clean section
- **Ventilate** — open garage or run a fan, this stuff evaporates fast but it’s still super flammable and vapor-heavy
- Keep away from any open flame, grinders, or sparks nearby (🔥💥)
- Let it fully **dry before torch or grinder** touches it (won't take long)

### 🌱 TL;DR for **Photon Flux** in Grow Lights:
> **Photon flux = how many light particles (photons) are hitting the plant every second**

Let’s break it all down like a nutrient chart:

---

### ⚡ Voltage & Current (Electrical Side)
- **Voltage (V):** How hard the electricity is pushing
- **Current (A):** How much electricity is flowing
- Together = how much **power** the grow light is drawing (watts)

But this only tells you how much energy the light is *using*, not what the plant *receives*.

---

### 🌈 PAR (Photosynthetically Active Radiation)
- PAR = the **spectrum of light** plants can use (400–700nm)  
- Think of it as the **"usable color range"** for photosynthesis  
- PAR isn’t a number — it’s a **range of wavelengths**

---

### 🔆 **PPF (Photosynthetic Photon Flux)**
> The **number of usable light photons emitted per second**, measured in **μmol/s**

- This is your **"how much grow fuel"** is coming from the light
- Higher PPF = more light the plant can absorb for photosynthesis
- PPF is NOT affected by voltage or current directly — it depends on **LED efficiency and diode design**

---

### 🪞 **PPFD (Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density)**
> PPF **+ area** = **how much hits your plant per square meter**, measured in **μmol/m²/s**

- THIS is the number that matters at **plant level**
- It's like **“light pressure”** on the leaf surface
- Too low = slow growth  
- Too high = light burn or wasted photons

---

### ⚙️ Putting It Together:
| Term        | Stands For                            | Measured In     | Tells You                        |
|-------------|----------------------------------------|------------------|----------------------------------|
| Voltage     | Electric push                         | Volts (V)        | How hard electricity flows       |
| Current     | Electric flow                         | Amps (A)         | How much electricity             |
| PAR         | Usable spectrum range                 | 400–700nm        | What kind of light is usable     |
| **PPF**     | Photosynthetic Photon Flux            | μmol/s           | How many usable light particles |
| **PPFD**    | Photon Flux Density (per area)        | μmol/m²/s        | How much hits the plant          |
