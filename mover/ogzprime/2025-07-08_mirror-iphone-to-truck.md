#### **1. Wireless HDMI dongle + HDMI interface**
If your factory deck has (or can be adapted to) an **HDMI input**, this is clean.

**What you’ll need:**
- ✅ [**Apple Lightning to HDMI adapter**](https://www.apple.com/shop/product/MD826AM/A/lightning-digital-av-adapter) (official – don’t cheap out, knockoffs cause flickering)
- ✅ **HDMI input interface** for your truck’s stereo (make sure it’s vehicle-specific—some brands like iSimple, GROM, Beat-Sonic make them)
- ✅ Optional: **Wireless casting dongle** (like AirFly, AnyCast, MiraScreen, or even Apple TV mini-style box if you’ve got cabin power)

**How it works:**
Plug Lightning into the adapter → HDMI to interface → Interface converts signal for your truck screen → Boom. iPhone mirrored.

---

#### **2. RCA/AV Input Adapter (for older head units)**
If you don’t have HDMI but **do have RCA (yellow/red/white)** or a video input:

**Setup:**
- Lightning to HDMI → HDMI to RCA converter → RCA into truck video-in → Input select on stereo → Done.

**Notes:**
- Video quality will drop. Text will be kinda rough.
- Good enough for maps or streaming, not good for reading small print.

---

#### **3. Screen-Mirroring Modules for iOS**
Plug-and-play boxes that mimic CarPlay behavior **without needing actual CarPlay**.

Brands like:
- **Carlinkit Autokit** (make sure it supports “iOS Mirroring” without CarPlay)
- **Ottocast** (some newer ones allow mirroring mode)
- **Naviks** or **GROM VLine** modules (for specific vehicles)

You’d run a USB or AV input to the box → phone connects wirelessly or wired → it spoofs CarPlay/mirroring → you see your screen.

---

### ⚠️ Avoid These:
- **Fake CarPlay dongles** – they only show compatible apps, not full mirroring
- **Knockoff Lightning to HDMI adapters** – 90% of them drop out, get hot, or brick during updates
- **Bluetooth-only adapters** – BT doesn’t carry video.
