### Key Factors to Consider

- **Corrosion Resistance:** Saltwater is harsh on gear. Look for rods with corrosion-resistant components such as stainless steel guides and reel seats designed for saltwater environments.
- **Rod Action & Power:**  
  - **Fast Action:** Offers sensitivity and quick hooksets—ideal for species that require a rapid strike.  
  - **Moderate to Heavy Power:** Necessary if you're targeting larger or tougher fish.
- **Length:** Onshore saltwater rods typically range between 7 to 10 feet. A longer rod can improve casting distance but might sacrifice some precision.
- **Compatibility:** Ensure your rod matches well with your reel, particularly a saltwater spinning reel designed to handle corrosive environments.
