---

### 🔍 Spot Diagnosis (Based on That Photo):
**1. Leaf Spot Disease (Fungal/Bacterial)**  
- **Look:** Irregular brown/purple splotches, sometimes surrounded by yellow halos.
- **Cause:** Warm, humid conditions + poor airflow. Spores love wet leaves.
- **Fix:**
  - Prune infected leaves (trash, don’t compost).
  - Improve airflow (thin out surrounding plants if needed).
  - Use a copper-based fungicide or neem oil spray as a preventative.

**2. Overwatering / Poor Drainage**
- **Look:** Soft spots, yellowing, leaf curling, and black edges.
- **Check:** Feel that soil. Is it soggy AF? Let it dry out between waterings.
- **Fix:** Raise drainage game. Make sure that pot drains fast and clear.

**3. Nutrient Deficiency (Usually Potassium or Magnesium)**
- **Look:** Spots + leaf curling or yellowing from the edges in.
- **Fix:** Hit it with a balanced fertilizer (especially one with micronutrients) or add a dash of Epsom salt (magnesium sulfate) diluted in water (1 tbsp/gal) once every couple weeks.

**4. Sunburn / Heat Stress**
- **Look:** Scorched-looking patches on upper leaves. Often crispy brown.
- **Fix:** If it’s been in direct Texas-style sunlight too long, maybe give it a bit of partial shade during peak hours.

---

### 💡 Quick Checklist for Recovery:
- ✅ Remove affected leaves.
- ✅ Let the soil dry slightly if it’s moist.
- ✅ Apply neem oil or copper fungicide (morning or evening, not in full sun).
- ✅ Feed it a balanced fertilizer.
- ✅ Watch for pests — because sometimes they come after a stressed plant.
