## 🎯 **Training Title/Theme:**  
**"Mission Possible: Leading School Success Through Data, Planning, and People"**  
*Your mission, should you choose to accept it... is to transform your CNA, DIP, and CIPs from compliance checklists into strategic roadmaps for real change.*

---

## 🧭 **Session Overview: Total Time – 4.5 hours (with breaks)**

### **🔹 Part 1: Welcome + Setting the Mission (30 minutes)**
- **Theme Music Intro:** *Mission Impossible theme* (or another energizing track)
- **Icebreaker:** *“Name that Data!”* — Show random charts or data snippets and ask what kind of data it is (attendance, STAAR, etc.)
- **Quick Poll:** Ask attendees what their biggest struggle is with CNA/DIP/CIP (use Mentimeter or sticky notes)

---

### **🔹 Part 2: Mission Intelligence — Analyzing CNA and CIPs (60 minutes)**  
> **Objective:** Review current CNA and CIPs to identify strengths, gaps, and compliance issues.

- **Interactive Review:**  
  - Break into small groups: Each gets a section of the CNA or a CIP to review for:
    - ✳️ Strengths
    - 🚨 Weaknesses
    - ⚖️ Compliance red flags
  - Use a checklist: “CNA & CIP Quick Look Rubric” (you’ll provide)
  - Gallery Walk: Groups post findings around the room, rotate, and comment

- **Discussion:**  
  - Common strengths/weaknesses across campuses?
  - Are plans actionable or vague?

---

### **🔹 Part 3: Mission Data — Identifying Needs, Root Causes & Prioritization (60 minutes)**  
> **Objective:** Deep dive into data and practice crafting problem statements and identifying root causes.

- **Mini-Lecture:** Types of Data to Use  
  - Quantitative: STAAR, attendance, discipline, etc.  
  - Qualitative: Surveys, focus groups, etc.

- **Group Activity: “The Problem Behind the Problem”**  
  - Present 2 real data sets from the district (or mock data if needed)
  - Groups write:
    - Problem statement (objective, not emotional)
    - Root cause(s)
    - Priority level (High/Med/Low)
  - Use a template: Problem → Root Cause → Need → Priority

- **Tool Demo:** Root Cause Fishbone Diagram or 5 Whys method

---

### **💡 BREAK (10 minutes) — with snacks or “mission fuel”**

---

### **🔹 Part 4: From Problems to Plans — Goals, Objectives & Strategies (60 minutes)**  
> **Objective:** Use problem statements to write goals, SMART objectives, and aligned strategies.

- **Mini-Lecture:** What makes a SMART objective SMART?
- **Group Work:**  
  - You provide 3 problem statements
  - Groups build:  
    - 🎯 Broad goal  
    - ✅ SMART objective  
    - 🔧 Strategy/Activity aligned to CNA data
  - Present 1 plan back to group

- **Resource Handout:** Goal/Objective/Strategy builder template

---

### **🔹 Part 5: Plan Like a Pro — CNA Summary & SBDM Planning (45 minutes)**  
> **Objective:** Prepare summary statements and stakeholder engagement action steps.

- **CNA Summary Tips:**  
  - Include top 3 needs  
  - Explain root causes briefly  
  - Set up the rationale for goals in DIP/CIP

- **SBDM Simulation Game:**  
  - Roleplay a SBDM meeting  
  - Assign roles: Principal, parent, teacher, student rep, etc.  
  - Scenario: Present new CNA findings and proposed DIP goal. Get feedback.

- **Action Steps Handout for Principals:**  
  - Prepping for SBDM  
  - Meeting structure  
  - Gathering feedback  
  - Closing the loop

---

### **🔹 Part 6: Mission Debrief & Celebration (15 minutes)**  
- **Review Mission Checklist:**
  - ✅ Analyzed CNA/CIPs  
  - ✅ Identified data sources  
  - ✅ Built problem statements  
  - ✅ Wrote goals, objectives, strategies  
  - ✅ Outlined stakeholder action steps  

- **Exit Ticket:**  
  - “What’s one thing you’ll do differently tomorrow?” (Sticky note or online padlet)

- **Swag or Fun Prize Drawings:**  
  - Small themed prizes: “Most likely to crack the root cause,” “Best SMART objective,” etc.

- **Closing Slide:**  
  > *“Remember: Plans don’t change outcomes — people with a plan do.”*
