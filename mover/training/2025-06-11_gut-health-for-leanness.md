### 🧬 What Are “Lean Bacteria”?

Research consistently shows that *lean people* tend to have higher levels of:
- **Akkermansia muciniphila** – linked to improved insulin sensitivity and reduced inflammation.
- **Faecalibacterium prausnitzii** – produces butyrate, a short-chain fatty acid (SCFA) that reduces gut permeability and inflammation.
- **Bifidobacteria & Lactobacillus** – classic probiotics, help digest fiber and modulate immunity.

People who are obese, insulin-resistant, or inflamed tend to have **lower microbial diversity** and fewer of these beneficial strains, with more of the "energy-harvesting" Firmicutes strains.

- Gut microbes produce **neurotransmitters** like serotonin and GABA.
- Poor gut health = increased intestinal permeability (“leaky gut”) → systemic inflammation → brain fog, joint pain, depression.
- Some *autoimmune diseases* and even mental illnesses may stem from dysbiosis.
