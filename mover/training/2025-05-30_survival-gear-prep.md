### 🧃 **Hydration & Electrolytes**
- **3 Stages of Dehydration**:
  1. Dizziness / cold nose / dry lips / slurred thinking
  2. Rapid heart rate
  3. Loss of consciousness / tremors

- **Electrolyte Imbalance**:
  - Na/K/Mg/Ca balance affects nerve conduction & heart
  - Use ORS (Oral Rehydration Salts) or coconut water

---

### 🍽️ **Nutrition / Fasting**
- **Refeed protocol** after 3-day fast:
  - Eat 3 weeks of clean food before long fasts
  - Reintroduce sodium + potassium gradually
  - Avoid processed carbs initially
- After 3 days: body triggers autophagy and cleanup
- Avoid long water-only fasts unless trained

---

### 🧠 **Mental & Relational Prep**
- Surround yourself with clear-minded people
- Cut off sources of mental sabotage
- Be prepared to lead or support family
- Be useful, not just present
