# Freemax Wattage Guide

**Assistant** (2025-04-09T19:38:17.901219):
Finding the “sweet spot” for flavor and smoothness on a Freemax tank largely depends on which exact Freemax coil you’re using and your personal preference. However, there are a few general guidelines that help most vapers using 3 mg e-liquid strike a balance between strong flavor and minimal harshness:

---

## 1. Know Your Coil’s Recommended Range
Each Freemax coil (e.g., Single Mesh, Double Mesh, Triple Mesh, etc.) has a recommended wattage range printed on the coil or listed in the user manual. This is the best starting point. For instance:

- **Single Mesh Coil** usually has a recommended range of around **40–70 W**.  
- **Double (Dual) Mesh Coil** often recommends **60–90 W**.  
- **Triple Mesh Coil** might recommend around **80–110 W**.  

These numbers can vary slightly depending on the exact coil series, but they’ll point you in the right direction.

---

## 2. Start at the Lower End
To minimize harshness (especially with a 3 mg e-liquid which can still give a throat hit if you push the wattage too high), start at the lower end of the coil’s recommended range:

- **Single Mesh**: Begin around **40–45 W** and work up.  
- **Dual Mesh**: Begin around **60–65 W** and adjust.  
- **Triple Mesh**: Start around **80 W** and fine-tune.  

Slowly raise the wattage until you find your preferred balance of warmth, vapor production, and flavor. If you start to sense dryness or a harsher inhale, dial the wattage back slightly.

---

## 3. Watch for Flavor and Heat
- **Flavor** tends to be best somewhere in the mid-range. If you go too high, you risk scorching the coil or getting dry hits.  
- **Heat** increases as wattage goes up, which can cause the vape to feel harsher. If you find it too intense, lower the wattage a bit.

---

## 4. Adjust Based on E-Liquid Composition
- **VG/PG Ratio**: High-VG liquids (e.g., 70/30) are smoother on the throat but can mute flavor if vaped too cool. You might prefer slightly higher wattages if your juice is thick (high VG), but you don’t want to overdo it if you’re sensitive to heat.  
- **Nicotine Strength**: At 3 mg, you won’t have as pronounced a throat hit as higher strengths, so it’s usually safe to push wattage a bit without getting too harsh. Still, if you notice any harshness, you can scale back.

---

## 5. Give the Coil a Chance to Break In
When you install a new coil, it’s normal to experience a slight break-in period (the first 10–20 draws). During that time, stay near the lower end of the wattage range to let the coil wick properly and gradually settle into a clean, flavorful vape. If you jump straight to the upper wattage limit on a fresh coil, you risk burning the wick prematurely.

---

## Example Starting Points
- **Single Mesh:** Begin at 45 W → move up in 5 W increments → find your sweet spot (often 50–60 W for many vapers).  
- **Dual Mesh:** Start at 60–65 W → move up slowly → many vapers prefer 65–75 W.  
- **Triple Mesh:** Begin at 80 W → move up in increments → you’ll often land in the 85–95 W zone.

---

### Final Tips
1. **Pace Yourself**: Take shorter pulls if you sense warmth building up or harshness.  
2. **Hydration**: Staying hydrated can help reduce any throat irritation.  
3. **Replace Coils Promptly**: Once flavor drops off or harshness suddenly spikes, it can be a sign the coil is spent.

By starting low and working your way up in small steps, you’ll be able to home in on the wattage that delivers the best flavor for your specific coil and juice. Good luck with finding that perfect balance!
