### 🧍 Step-by-Step Breakdown

**1. Stance & Setup**
- **Position:** Stand upright with knees slightly bent.
- **Ball Placement:** Hold the ball at waist level with both hands.
- **Focus:** Keep your eyes on your target arrow down the lane.

**2. First Step (Pushaway)**
- **Movement:** Step forward with your right foot (for right-handed bowlers).
- **Action:** Simultaneously push the ball out and down into the swing path.

**3. Second Step (Downswing)**
- **Movement:** Step with your left foot.
- **Action:** Allow the ball to swing naturally downward; your non-bowling arm extends to the side for balance.

**4. Third Step (Backswing)**
- **Movement:** Step with your right foot.
- **Action:** The ball reaches the peak of the backswing behind you.

**5. Fourth Step (Slide & Release)**
- **Movement:** Slide forward with your left foot.
- **Action:** As the ball swings forward, release it near your ankle. Ensure your hand is behind the ball with a straight wrist for a straight shot or rotate your hand slightly for a hook.

**6. Follow-Through**
- **Action:** After releasing the ball, continue your arm swing upward towards your target, finishing with your hand at shoulder height. This promotes accuracy and balance.

---

### 🎯 Additional Tips

- **Consistency:** Maintain the same starting position and approach each time to build muscle memory.
- **Relaxation:** Keep your grip firm but relaxed to allow a smooth swing.
- **Visualization:** Before your approach, visualize the ball's path down the lane to your target.
