Ah, the mystical rules of Pig Latin — the unofficial language of mischief.

When a word **starts with a vowel** (A, E, I, O, U), the rule is super simple:

**You just add “-way” or “-yay” to the end of the word.**  

So:

- *apple* becomes **appleway** or **appleyay**
- *orange* becomes **orangeway** or **orangeyay**

**Both versions are technically acceptable**, but "way" is more traditional in older schoolyard Pig Latin, while "yay" shows up in more casual or creative versions.

## **The Quantum Pop Cupcake**  
**Flavor:** Lemon cake with raspberry frosting  
**Surprise Center:** Raspberry jam & white chocolate core  
**Mood:** Light like a starbeam. Punchy like a boss battle. Sweet like a win screen.

---

### **Ingredients** (makes 12 cupcakes):

#### **Cupcakes (Lemon Cake):**
- 1½ cups all-purpose flour  
- 1 tsp baking powder  
- ¼ tsp baking soda  
- ¼ tsp salt  
- ½ cup unsalted butter (room temp)  
- ¾ cup granulated sugar  
- 2 large eggs  
- 1½ tsp pure vanilla extract  
- Zest of 2 lemons  
- ¼ cup fresh lemon juice  
- ½ cup buttermilk (or milk + 1 tsp vinegar)

#### **Filling (Surprise Pop Core):**
- ½ cup raspberry jam  
- 2 oz white chocolate, chopped fine and stirred into the jam until melted

#### **Frosting (Raspberry Buttercream):**
- 1 cup unsalted butter (room temp)  
- 3 cups powdered sugar  
- ¼ cup raspberry puree (fresh or from frozen)  
- 1 tsp vanilla  
- Pinch of salt

---

### **Instructions:**

#### **1. Cupcakes:**
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a cupcake tray.
2. In one bowl: whisk flour, baking powder, soda, salt.
3. In another: cream butter + sugar until light. Add eggs, one at a time.
4. Mix in lemon zest, juice, and vanilla.
5. Alternate adding dry ingredients and buttermilk until smooth.
6. Fill cupcake liners ¾ full and bake 18–20 mins. Cool completely.

#### **2. Surprise Center:**
- Once cupcakes are cool, use a knife or cupcake corer to remove a little plug from the center.
- Spoon in about a teaspoon of the **raspberry-white chocolate filling**.
- Replace the cake plug on top (no one will know it’s been modified... stealth mission complete).

#### **3. Raspberry Frosting:**
1. Beat butter till fluffy. Add powdered sugar a little at a time.
2. Add vanilla, raspberry puree, and salt. Whip until creamy and pink like a hero’s blush.

#### **4. Assemble & Decorate:**
- Pipe a swirl of frosting on top.
- Add sprinkles, edible glitter, or a candied lemon slice.
- Optional: Top with a mini white chocolate star or a secret edible code word like “OGZ”.
