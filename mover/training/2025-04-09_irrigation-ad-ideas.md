- **Grinders/sanders:** Usually **8–15 CFM** at 90 PSI.

### **Ideal Compressor Specs for Versatility:**
- **Tank:** 30 gallons minimum (60 gallons strongly recommended for smooth, uninterrupted usage).
- **Airflow (CFM):** At least 8–12 CFM at 90 PSI to comfortably run most paint sprayers and air tools.

### **Summary (Optimal Recommendation):**

- **Ideal:** 60-gallon tank, 10–15+ CFM at 90 PSI for smooth operation of all typical air tools and spray equipment without frustrating interruptions.

Choose your compressor based on the most demanding tool you'll use regularly—usually paint sprayers or grinders. Bigger tanks mean less downtime and smoother operation.
