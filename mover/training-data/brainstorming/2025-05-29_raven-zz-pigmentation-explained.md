**Assistant** (2025-05-29T19:58:23.778384):
The **'Raven ZZ' plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia 'Raven')** appears black due to a **high concentration of anthocyanin pigments**, not variegation.

Here’s the breakdown:

---

### 🖤 Why 'Raven ZZ' Looks Black:
- **Young leaves** start out **bright green**.
- As they mature, they **darken to a deep purple-black**.
- This darkening is caused by **anthocyanins**, the same pigments that give blueberries, black rice, and purple cabbage their color.

---

### ❌ Is it variegation?
No — *variegation* refers to leaves having **multiple colors or patterns** due to:
- **Genetic mutations** (chimeral or stable variegation)
- **Lack of chlorophyll** in certain parts
- **Viral infections** (rarely intentional)

Raven ZZ doesn't have multiple patterns — it has a **uniform, solid dark color**, so it’s **not variegated**.

---

### 🌱 Summary:
- **Color source**: Anthocyanins, not variegation
- **Trait type**: Genetically stable pigmentation
- **Care**: Similar to standard ZZs, but enjoys **bright indirect light** to bring out the deepest coloration (though it tolerates low light)
