---

### ✂️ Step-by-Step Plant Split Surgery

#### **1. Remove It from the Pot**
- Gently tilt and wiggle it out. That root mass is going to be **chonky**.
- Don’t yank by the stems — hold the base.

#### **2. Shake or Rinse Off Some Soil**
- You want to see the thick **rhizomes** (potato-like tubers under the soil).
- This will help you find natural “seams” where the plant clumps can separate.

#### **3. Separate by Clumps**
- Look for **natural groupings** — each stem cluster usually has its own rhizome.
- Gently pull apart with your hands. If they resist, cut them apart with a sterilized blade, aiming to keep:
  - At least 1–2 stems
  - A chunk of rhizome
  - Some roots per division

> ⚠️ **Important**: If any rhizomes are **mushy or brown**, cut those out. Healthy ones are firm and white/yellowish.

#### **4. Repot the Divisions**
- Use clean pots with good drainage.
- Plant them shallow — don’t bury the rhizomes deep.
- Press soil around the base gently but firmly.

#### **5. Post-Op Recovery**
- **Do NOT water immediately.** Let them sit dry for 3–5 days to callous over and avoid root rot.
- Then resume light watering — small amounts.
- Place them in **indirect bright light** (not full sun yet).

---

### 👶 Bonus: Propagate from Leaves Too
- You can also take healthy leaves and root them in water or soil (slow process, but works).
- Just pluck a leaf cleanly and let it sit out for a day before placing it in slightly moist soil.
