**Assistant** (2025-07-29T20:28:49.869185):
Alright Trey, let’s shut some of that startup clutter down and get your Mac booting like it’s got somewhere to be. Here’s how you change your startup programs on macOS:

---

### 🔧 **Method 1: System Settings (macOS Ventura and later)**

1. **Click the Apple logo** in the top-left corner.
2. Select **System Settings**.
3. Go to **General > Login Items**.
4. Under **Open at Login**:
   - See a list of apps that auto-start.
   - Hit the **minus (-)** button to **remove** an app.
   - Hit the **plus (+)** button to **add** one back (if you like chaos, I guess).

5. Under **Allow in Background**, you’ll see apps that run sneakily. You can toggle these off if they don’t need to be doing... whatever it is they think they’re doing.

---

### 🧰 **Method 2: Old School Style (macOS Monterey and earlier)**

1. Apple logo > **System Preferences**.
2. Go to **Users & Groups**.
3. Select your user account.
4. Click the **Login Items** tab.
5. Same drill: select apps and click the **minus (-)** to remove.

---

### 💣 Bonus: Kill Lingering Daemons Like a Mac Ninja

For more advanced users (and you seem like that type):

- Go to **~/Library/LaunchAgents/** or **/Library/LaunchAgents/** or **LaunchDaemons**.
- You’ll find `.plist` files — these are startup scripts.
- Deleting or disabling the wrong one can mess things up, so **only remove stuff you recognize** (e.g., Adobe updater, not your audio drivers).

---

### 🧼 One More Thing: Clean Up Utility Tools

If you’re into third-party tools:
- **CleanMyMac X**, **AppCleaner**, or **Startupizer** can give you more visual control over login items, background processes, and scheduled tasks.
