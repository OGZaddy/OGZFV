# **How to Replace the Coil on a Freemax (any tank: Mesh Pro, Fireluke, M Pro 2, etc.)**

## 1. **Unscrew the Tank from the Mod**
- Grip the tank (the part you put juice in) and twist it **counter-clockwise** off the battery/mod.
- If it's sticky from juice, grab a paper towel for grip — no need to Hulk smash it.

## 2. **Disassemble the Tank**
- Twist off the **bottom base** of the tank (counter-clockwise again).
- Now you should be staring at the **coil** — it’s usually **screwed into the base**.
  
  **(Looks like a little metal barrel with holes and cotton showing.)**

## 3. **Remove the Old Coil**
- Simply **unscrew the coil** from the tank base.
- If it's stuck, **use a rubber glove or towel** to get better grip and torque it out.

## 4. **Prime Your New Coil** *(this is CRITICAL — don't skip!)*
- Grab a **new coil**.
- **Drip a few drops of your vape juice directly onto the exposed cotton** (top and side holes) to "prime" it.
  - **Goal:** Saturate the cotton so you don't get a nasty burnt taste on your first hit.
  
  > Think of it like "soaking a sponge before you microwave it" — dry cotton burns bad.

## 5. **Install the New Coil**
- Screw the new coil **snugly** into the tank base.
  - Don’t over-tighten — just **firmly finger-tight** is good.
  
## 6. **Reassemble the Tank**
- Screw the base (with the new coil installed) back onto the tank.

## 7. **Fill the Tank with Juice**
- Open the top fill port.
- Squeeze your e-liquid into the fill hole (not down the center airflow pipe! That floods the coil).

## 8. **Wait 5–10 Minutes**
- Let the juice fully soak into the new coil before firing.
  - **THIS IS THE "Patience = No Burnt Hits" rule.**
  
  **Bonus:** You can take a few "primer puffs" without hitting the fire button if you want to speed it up.

## 9. **Fire it Up**
- Screw the tank back onto your mod.
- Set it to a **moderate wattage** (check the side of your coil, it’ll say like "Recommended 60–80W" or something).
- Start on the **lower end** and work up.
