You've got a **Freemax Fireluke Mesh Pro tank** in the picture — a solid sub-ohm tank. Here's how to load (fill) it up and get it ready to vape:

### **Step-by-Step: Filling Your Tank**
1. **Locate the red dot on the top cap**  
   On the metal ring near the drip tip (mouthpiece), you’ll see a red dot — that's your sliding point.

2. **Slide the top cap**  
   Push the top cap at the red dot in the direction of the arrow (usually backward or to the side). It’ll reveal a fill port.

3. **Add your e-liquid**  
   Tilt your juice bottle and insert the nozzle into the exposed fill port — **don’t pour into the center chimney**, just the side slot.

4. **Fill slowly** to avoid overflow or air bubbles.

5. **Slide the cap back into place**  
   Once it’s filled, slide the top cap back until it clicks shut.

---

### **Before You Vape**
- If you’ve **just installed a new coil**, **let it sit for 5–10 minutes** after filling so the cotton inside the coil can fully soak up the e-liquid. This is called **priming the coil** and helps prevent burning it out.

- Optionally, you can prime the coil manually by placing a few drops of e-liquid directly onto the cotton through the coil openings before installing it.

### **Quick Refill for Your Freemax Fireluke Tank:**

1. **Find the red dot on the top cap** (just under the drip tip).
2. **Slide the top cap open** in the direction of the red dot.
3. **Insert the nozzle of your juice bottle** into the exposed fill hole (not the center hole).
4. **Fill slowly** until the tank is about 80–90% full.
5. **Slide the top cap shut** until it clicks.
6. **Wait 1–2 minutes** if it's been dry or sitting for a bit, just to re-saturate the coil.

---

You're good to vape after that! If it gurgles a bit or spits juice, just give it a gentle blow through the mouthpiece while firing it for a second — that usually clears any excess.
