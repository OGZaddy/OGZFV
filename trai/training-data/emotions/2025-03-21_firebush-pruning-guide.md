This plant appears to be a **Firebush** (*Hamelia patens*), a fast-growing, heat-tolerant shrub known for its vibrant red-orange tubular flowers that attract hummingbirds and butterflies. It is commonly grown in warm climates and thrives in full sun to partial shade.

### Can You Prune Firebush Aggressively?
Yes, **Firebush can tolerate aggressive pruning** and will bounce back quickly. Here are some pruning tips:

1. **Hard Pruning (Rejuvenation Pruning):** In early spring or late winter, you can cut it back significantly (down to 12-18 inches from the ground) to encourage fresh, vigorous growth.
2. **Shaping & Maintenance:** Regular trimming helps maintain a compact shape and prevents it from becoming too leggy.
3. **Deadheading:** Removing spent flowers can encourage more blooms throughout the growing season.
4. **Cold Climate Considerations:** If you are in a zone where frost occurs, Firebush may die back in winter but will regrow from the base in spring.
