If you're using an **iPhone** or **Android**, there are a few ways to copy and paste a bunch of texts easily:

### **For iPhone (iMessage)**
1. **Long-press a message** in the conversation.
2. Tap **"More"**, then select multiple messages.
3. Tap the **"Forward"** icon (arrow in the bottom right).
4. Instead of sending, **press and hold** in the message box and select **"Select All"** → **"Copy"**.
5. Paste it here.

**Alternative Method (Mac)**
- If you're using a Mac with iMessage, you can **select multiple messages**, copy, and paste them here.

---

### **For Android (Google Messages/Samsung Messages)**
1. **Long-press a message**, then tap other messages to select multiple.
2. Tap **"Copy"** and paste it here.
   - If "Copy" isn’t available, tap **"Share"**, send it to yourself (Notes or Email), and copy from there.

**Alternative Method (Desktop)**
- If you have **Google Messages for Web**:  
  1. Open [messages.google.com/web](https://messages.google.com/web).
  2. Log in by scanning the QR code with your phone.
  3. Copy and paste directly from your computer.
