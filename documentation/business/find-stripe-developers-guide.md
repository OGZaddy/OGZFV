# 🔍 SIMPLE GUIDE: Finding Stripe "Developers" Section

## 🎯 **STEP BY STEP:**

### **1. Are you logged in?**
- Go to: https://dashboard.stripe.com
- Make sure you're **logged into your Stripe account** (the one with your API keys)

### **2. Look at the LEFT SIDEBAR**
Once logged in, you should see a **vertical menu on the left side**:

```
🏠 Home
💳 Payments  
🛍️ Products
👥 Customers
📊 Analytics
📋 Reports
🛠️ Developers  ← **THIS IS WHAT YOU NEED!**
⚙️ Settings
```

### **3. What if you DON'T see "Developers"?**

**Possible reasons:**
- ❌ Not logged in to the right account
- ❌ Account has restricted permissions
- ❌ Using a business account where you're not an admin

**Solutions:**
1. **Log out completely** and log back in
2. **Check you're using the RIGHT Stripe account** (the one where you got your API keys from)
3. **Try refreshing the page**
4. **Make sure you're not in "restricted view"**

### **4. Still can't find it?**

**Alternative method:**
1. Go to: https://dashboard.stripe.com/developers
2. This should take you directly to the developers section

### **5. Once you find "Developers":**
1. Click **"Developers"** in the left sidebar
2. Then click **"Webhooks"** 
3. Then **"Add endpoint"**

---

## 🔧 **If you STILL can't find it:**

**Tell me exactly what you see in your left sidebar when logged into Stripe.** 

The Developers section should be very visible - it has a **🛠️ wrench/tool icon** next to it.
